## Grammar Notes (Грамматика)

1. **Passive voice for operations**
   - *Derricks **are operated by** winches.*
   - *Cargo **is loaded** through hatches.*
   - *Anchors **are raised** by the windlass.*

2. **Imperative for orders/instructions (SMCP)**
   - *Stand by mooring winches forward and aft.*
   - *Open cargo hatches.*
   - *Start the ballast pump.*

3. **Present Simple for equipment function**
   - *Winches **operate** mooring lines.*
   - *The rudder **steers** the ship.*
   - *Pumps **transfer** ballast water.*

4. **Relative clauses for definitions**
   - *A winch **which is** used for anchors is called a windlass.*
   - *The space **where** cargo is stowed is the hold.*
