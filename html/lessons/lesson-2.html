Grammar Notes — Грамматика

1. Passive voice in instructions/descriptions

Subject + be + Past Participle (V3)

  • The hull is divided into compartments.
  • The propeller is bolted to the shaft.
  • Watertight doors are closed to prevent flooding.
  • Ballast water is pumped out regularly.

2. Modal verbs for obligation / necessity (SMCP style)

  • must — strong obligation: All watertight doors must be kept closed at sea.
  • should — recommendation: The lookout should be posted in restricted visibility.
  • can / could — ability: A bow thruster can turn the ship more rapidly.

3. Present Simple for general facts

  • Hulls have a pointed bow.
  • The rudder steers the ship.
  • Bulkheads form watertight compartments.
